## The three bands

| Chip | Crash point | What it means for a bet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| {{ chip(1.24) }} | Below 2× | A 2× target would have lost. |
| {{ chip(3.10) }} | 2× or more | A 2× target would have at least doubled the bet. |
| {{ chip(14.52) }} | 10× or more | A rare high round. |

The colour is always paired with the number, so the strip stays readable if you cannot tell the colours apart.

## What the strip does not tell you

{{ site.honesty_note }} The strip is a record of what happened. Each round's crash point is fixed before that round starts and does not depend on the rounds before it.

!!! warning "A common mistake"
    Several low rounds in a row do not mean a high round is "due".
