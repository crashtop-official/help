## What to check

Use the account’s communication tools for private messages. Do not send sensitive account or payment information in messages. Report requests for money through the reporting tools.

## Need help?

Contact [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}). Include the round ID or transaction identifier when relevant. Never send passwords, one-time codes, or recovery keys.
