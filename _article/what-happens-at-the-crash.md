## The moment of the crash

The multiplier stops and the badge above the chart changes to {{ badge("crashed") }}. The final value is added to the history strip.

## What happens to each bet

| Your bet was | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Cashed out before the crash | Paid at the multiplier you cashed out at. |
| Still open | Lost. |
| Queued for the next round | Not affected. It plays in the next round. |

## After the crash

A new betting window opens and the round's server seed is revealed. The finished round can be checked at any time: see [Verify a finished round]({{ url('/articles/verify-a-finished-round/') }}).
