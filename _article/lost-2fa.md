## What to check

First check your recovery options or backup codes. If access cannot be restored, request recovery only through official support. Removing the second factor should require verification of account ownership.

## Need help?

Contact [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}). Include the round ID or transaction identifier when relevant. Never send passwords, one-time codes, or recovery keys.
