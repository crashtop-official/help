## What to check

Obtain the deposit address and network directly from your account. Before sending, check asset and network compatibility, the minimum amount, and any memo or tag requirement. Transfers on the wrong network may be unrecoverable.

## Need help?

Contact [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}). Include the round ID or transaction identifier when relevant. Never send passwords, one-time codes, or recovery keys.
