## What to check

When reporting a bug, include reproduction steps, the time of the incident, relevant identifiers, and the expected result. Do not disclose confidential user information. Report vulnerabilities through the designated private channel.

## Need help?

Contact [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}). Include the round ID or transaction identifier when relevant. Never send passwords, one-time codes, or recovery keys.
