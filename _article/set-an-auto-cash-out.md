## Set a target

1. In the bet panel, find the **Auto cash-out** field.
2. Enter the multiplier you want to cash out at, for example `2.00`.
3. Place your bet as usual.

When the multiplier reaches your target, the bet is cashed out at exactly that value.

## If the round crashes first

If the round crashes below your target, the bet is lost, the same as any bet that was not cashed out.

## Cashing out by hand

An auto target does not lock you in. While the round is live you can still press **Cash out** before the target is reached.
