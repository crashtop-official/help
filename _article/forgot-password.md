## What to check

If your account has a recovery email, submit a request through the official recovery process and check your spam folder. Do not send passwords, one-time codes, or recovery keys to other people.

## Need help?

Contact [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}). Include the round ID or transaction identifier when relevant. Never send passwords, one-time codes, or recovery keys.
