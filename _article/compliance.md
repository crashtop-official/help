## Check the operator disclosures

Published legal information should identify the operator, place of registration, valid license, and complaint-handling authority precisely. Company registration, a data processing permit, and a gaming license are different things.

## Restrictions and verification

The terms should explain age restrictions, excluded regions, identity verification requirements, and dispute procedures. Technical access to a website does not establish that its use is lawful in a user’s location.

## Check current rules

This page is a content draft. Decisions about use or tax obligations require checking current local rules and the service’s final terms.
