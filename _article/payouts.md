## Track the payment stages

A withdrawal request, security confirmation, transaction broadcast, and network confirmation are separate stages. To track a withdrawal, compare its account status with the transaction identifier and network status.

## Check before confirming

Before confirming, check the net amount, fee, network, destination address, and any queue conditions. A displayed account balance alone is not proof of reserves.

## Fund custody

Descriptions of fund custody should distinguish online wallets, offline storage, and access controls. Only documented service features should be published.
