## Stakes and outcomes

In a crash game, a player selects a stake and a cash-out multiplier. A successful cash-out returns the stake multiplied by the multiplier; if the round ends before cash-out, the entire stake for that round can be lost.

## House edge and variance

The house edge describes a mathematical average over a large volume of bets. It does not determine the outcome of a session or each individual player’s loss. A higher target multiplier generally means a lower probability of success and a larger payout.

## Independent rounds

In a model with independent rounds, consecutive losses do not improve the chance of winning the next round. Increasing stakes to recover losses does not change the house edge and increases potential losses. History colors are not a prediction tool.
